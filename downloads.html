MSc

A Framework for Visualizing Detection of Failure in Simply Supported Reinforced Concrete Beam Structure (SSRCBS)

Master's research integrating Civil Engineering and Computer Science through an information-visualization framework for detecting failure in simply supported reinforced concrete beam structures.

The research developed a visualization-based reinforced concrete design environment using Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture, Visual Basic .NET and AutoCAD to support the analysis and visualization of reinforced concrete beam behaviour.

Degree: M.Sc. Computer Science Year: 2009 Institution: University of Agriculture, Abeokuta Research Area: Engineering Computing and Information Visualization
