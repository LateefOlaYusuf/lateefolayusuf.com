Profile

A career spanning engineering, public service and technology

Engr. Dr. Yusuf Lateef Oladimeji is a Civil Engineer, Computer Scientist and Procurement Specialist whose professional career combines infrastructure engineering, public-sector administration, project management, procurement and applied information technology.

He served in the Ogun State Civil Service for 35 years, beginning his career as Engineer II and progressing through senior engineering and administrative responsibilities before serving as Permanent Secretary in the Ogun State Ministry of Works and Infrastructure.

His professional experience includes road infrastructure, public works, project implementation, contract administration, procurement, construction supervision, institutional coordination and infrastructure policy implementation.

Current Focus

Research, software and knowledge transfer

Following retirement from public service, my current focus includes research, lecturing, professional consulting and software development, particularly where Civil Engineering and Computer Science intersect.

One of my current development initiatives is CivilWorks Manager, a construction project management application designed to support procurement, site management, cost control and project monitoring.
