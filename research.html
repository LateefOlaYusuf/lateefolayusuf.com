Research & Innovation

Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of Civil Engineering, Computer Science, infrastructure management, procurement, project delivery and digital transformation.

Interdisciplinary Focus

Connecting engineering practice with computing

Modern infrastructure delivery increasingly depends on reliable data, digital systems, automation, intelligent decision support and effective project governance. My research interests therefore bring engineering and computing together to address practical infrastructure and management challenges.

01

Digital Construction Management

Development of digital systems for planning, procurement, construction monitoring, reporting and control of civil engineering projects.

02

Infrastructure Information Systems

Application of information systems to infrastructure asset management, project databases, decision support and institutional knowledge management.

03

Artificial Intelligence in Civil Engineering

Investigation of intelligent and data-driven approaches for construction planning, risk assessment, prediction, monitoring and engineering decision-making.

04

Project Cost & Performance Analytics

Use of data analytics to improve cost control, schedule performance, project forecasting and infrastructure delivery outcomes.

05

Public Procurement & Contract Management

Research into procurement systems, contractor performance, contract administration and governance of public infrastructure projects.

06

Digital Transformation in Public Works

Design and adoption of digital tools that improve transparency, coordination, institutional efficiency and evidence-based infrastructure management.

Research Direction

The goal is not technology for its own sake, but technology that improves engineering decisions, accountability and project outcomes.

Research Questions

Problems of continuing interest

How can digital systems improve public infrastructure project delivery?

Exploring how integrated platforms can improve information flow, accountability, reporting and decision-making across project stakeholders.

How can project data support better forecasting?

Investigating methods for using historical and real-time project information to predict delays, cost overruns and implementation risks.

How can procurement performance be measured digitally?

Developing approaches for monitoring procurement milestones, contractor performance, compliance and contract implementation.

How can Civil Engineering and Computer Science reinforce one another?

Studying interdisciplinary approaches in which engineering expertise defines the real problem while computing provides scalable analytical and operational tools.
